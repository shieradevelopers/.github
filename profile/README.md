# 🌙 Shi’era Developers Team  

Welcome to the official GitHub organization of **Shi’era Developers Team** — a group of passionate **Muslim developers** united by faith, creativity, and purpose.

Shi’era Developers Team is more than just a developer community — it’s a **Muslim Jema’ah of innovation and knowledge**.  
Our mission is to **learn**, **build**, and **grow** together, contributing to the world through technology that benefits people and pleases Allah ﷻ.
